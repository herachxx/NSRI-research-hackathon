Event recap

Five days from question to research paper.

The fully virtual sprint ran July 13–18, followed by two rounds of review and a live Grand Finale on July 25. Every submission was reviewed against innovation, technical execution, impact, and communication.

How the results were decided

Review built for rigor.

The result archive separates the official ranking from finalist recognition: only the Top 25 are ranked; the other 75 appear alphabetically with no implied order.

01

Initial screening

Every submission was reviewed using the 120-point rubric. One hundred teams advanced.

02

Expert review

PhD-level and industry judges evaluated the Top 100 and selected 25 Grand Finalists.

03

Grand Finale

The Top 25 presented live. Final scores established the official ranked placements.
